The 10-Minute FBA Leak Checklist

Grab a coffee and open Seller Central. Go down this list once a month. Every box you can't tick is money that may be slipping away.

1. Reimbursements (lost & damaged units)

  • Check your reimbursement report for units marked lost or damaged in Amazon's warehouse.
  • For any lost or damaged unit with no matching reimbursement, open a case with proof (invoice or proof of ownership).

2. Missing inbound units

  • For each recent shipment, compare units you sent to units Amazon received.
  • If they don't match, open an investigation with your invoice, packing list, and tracking.

3. Fee overcharges

  • Look up the size and weight Amazon has on file for your top 5 sellers.
  • Measure one packaged unit yourself. If Amazon's numbers push it into a higher size tier, ask for a re-measure and a refund of the extra.

4. Customer returns

  • Match recent refunds to returns. Flag any refund where the item never came back.
  • Check returns marked "customer damaged" or "unsellable" that looked fine to you. Those may be claimable.

5. Storage & aged inventory

  • Sort your inventory by age. Anything close to the aged inventory surcharge needs a plan: reprice, remove, or liquidate.
  • Before October, clear slow stock. Monthly storage fees are higher Oct–Dec.

Deadlines to file claims change, so always check Seller Central for the current window before you file.
