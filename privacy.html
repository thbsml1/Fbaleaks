Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 23, 2026

At Fbaleaks.com ("we", "us", or "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your sensitive e-commerce data. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit our website or use our tools.

1. 100% Client-Side Report Processing

Our tools (the Report Checker, Leak Estimator, SKU triage, and rebuttal drafts) run entirely inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript. When you upload your Amazon Inventory Ledger, Reimbursements, or inventory reports:

  • Your files are never uploaded to our servers, GitHub, or any third-party database.
  • All data processing happens locally on your device. Once you close or refresh the tab, your report data is cleared.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate our services and deliver free resources:

  • Email Addresses: Collected only when you choose to sign up for free checklists, estimates, or guides. If you use the Leak Estimator's email option, the estimate you calculated is included with your signup.
  • Payment Data: Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your card numbers.
  • Usage Data: We use the Meta (Facebook) Pixel to measure visits, sign-ups, and purchases so we can understand which pages and posts are helpful. The Pixel may use cookies and share data such as pages viewed with Meta. You can limit this through your browser settings or your Facebook ad preferences.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to support operations:

  • Stripe: Secure checkout and payments.
  • FormSubmit: Receives checklist sign-ups and sends our welcome email.
  • Meta Pixel: Visit and conversion measurement.
  • Google Fonts: Loads the fonts used on our pages.
  • GitHub Pages: Hosts our static website files.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

4. Communications & Opt-Out

If you sign up, you may receive occasional tips and product offers. You can stop them anytime by replying "unsubscribe" to any email or writing to the address below. We'll remove you promptly.

5. We Don't Sell Your Data

We do not sell or rent your email address or any personal information.
