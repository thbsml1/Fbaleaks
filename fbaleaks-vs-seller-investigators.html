Where Seller Investigators is a good fit

If you do not want to touch the claims at all, they will do it, and you pay nothing until money comes back. For a seller with a big account and no spare time, giving up a share can be a fair trade.

Where Fbaleaks is a better fit

You see results in 60 seconds, not 48 hours. You keep 100% of what you recover. And you pay once, not a share of every recovery.

The trade is your time. Fbaleaks shows you what is wrong and drafts the letter, but you open the case yourself. Amazon's claim windows are short, so check often.

What 25% really costs

On a $4,000 recovery, 25% is $1,000. The share grows with every dollar they recover, for as long as you use them. Fbaleaks stays one flat price.
