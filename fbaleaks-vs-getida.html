Where Getida is a good fit

If you do not want to touch the claims at all, they will do it, and you pay nothing unless they recover money. For a seller with a big account and no spare time, giving up a share can be a fair trade.

Where Fbaleaks is a better fit

You keep 100% of what you recover. You do not give anyone access to your Seller Central account. And you pay once, not a share of every recovery.

The trade is your time. Fbaleaks shows you what is wrong and drafts the letter, but you open the case yourself. Most reimbursement claims now expire after about 60 days, so check often.

What 20-25% really costs

On a $4,000 recovery, 20% is $800 and 25% is $1,000. The share grows with every dollar they recover, for as long as you use them. Fbaleaks stays $29.99 once for the first 50 Founding Members, then $49.99 once.

Common questions

Does Fbaleaks connect to my Amazon account?
No. You download your own reports and upload them in your browser. Your files are never sent to Fbaleaks, Amazon, Meta, or even Google.

Does Fbaleaks take a percentage of what I recover?
No. It is $29.99 once for the first 50 Founding Members, then $49.99 once. You keep 100% of what you recover.

Does Getida need access to my Amazon account?
Yes. You connect your Seller Central account and they audit your FBA transactions.

How much does Getida charge?
Third-party reviews put the standard rate at 25% of what it recovers, with 20% on some partner offers. Check their site for current terms.

Is Fbaleaks affiliated with Getida?
No. Fbaleaks is independent.

Details about Getida come from its public pages and third-party reviews as of October 2026. Check their site for anything that has changed.
