About Fbaleaks

Fbaleaks is built and run by one person: an Amazon wholesale seller who got tired of watching small amounts of money quietly disappear. Lost units that never got reimbursed, fees that crept up, returns that cost twice. There was no simple, fair-priced way to check for it without handing over account access or paying a cut of everything recovered.

So I built the tool I wanted. You can reach me directly at thbsml1@gmail.com. A real person answers.

What it does

  • A free Report Checker and Leak Calculator that run in your browser.
  • A paid toolkit: Keep/Reprice/Kill triage for your SKUs and reimbursement letters for denied reimbursement claims.
  • Free guides on the common FBA leaks, written from a seller's point of view.

Your data stays yours

Nothing connects to your Seller Central account. You download your own reports and the tools read them inside your browser. Your files are not uploaded to Fbaleaks, Amazon, Meta, or Google. The privacy page lists every service the site uses.

What it can't do

  • The Report Checker shows possible gaps, not guaranteed claims.
  • Reimbursement letters are starting points. Amazon decides every claim, and no one can promise approval.
  • Fbaleaks is not affiliated with Amazon.com, Inc.

Pricing and the refund promise

The full toolkit is a one-time payment, with no subscription and no commission on what you recover. The first 50 Founding Members pay $29.99 for lifetime access, after that the price goes to $49.99. If Fbaleaks doesn't help you find at least what you paid, email me and I'll refund you the same day. Your bank may take a few days to show it.
