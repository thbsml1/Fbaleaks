Where ReimburseOps is a good fit

If your one question is "did Amazon underpay me on claims it already paid?", ReimburseOps is built around exactly that. If you are happy with a monthly plan, it is a fair choice.

Where Fbaleaks is a better fit

You pay once. At about $19 a month, a year of ReimburseOps is about $228. Fbaleaks is $9.99 once for the first 50 Founding Members, then $19.99 once.

You also get more than a reimbursement check. Fbaleaks tells you which SKUs are losing money, finds lost units Amazon never paid for, and drafts a rebuttal when Amazon denies a claim.

Neither of us files the claim for you

Both tools find the problem and leave the filing to you. If you want someone to file for you, read the Fbaleaks vs. Refunds Manager comparison. Mind the clock too: most reimbursement claims now expire after about 60 days.

Common questions

Does Fbaleaks connect to my Amazon account?
No. You download your own reports and upload them in your browser. Your files are never sent to Fbaleaks, Amazon, Meta, or even Google.

Does Fbaleaks take a percentage of what I recover?
No. It is $9.99 once for the first 50 Founding Members, then $19.99 once. You keep 100% of what you recover.

How much is ReimburseOps?
Third-party listings show about $19 a month for its paid plan. Check their site for current pricing.

Is Fbaleaks affiliated with ReimburseOps?
No. Fbaleaks is independent.

Details about ReimburseOps come from its public pages and third-party listings as of October 2026. Check their site for anything that has changed.
